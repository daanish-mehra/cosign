Test, score, limit.

An agent can be talked out of its money by a poisoned web page, a fake "pay to fix this error" response, or a note planted in its memory. Today, whoever sets its spending limit is guessing. Cosign replaces the guess with a measurement.

1

Crash test

Your agent gets a funded wallet and normal jobs. We attack it through web pages, paid APIs, token pages, tool replies and memory, rewriting each attack until it lands or the agent holds. We judge only where the money went.

2

Score and fixes

A 0 to 100 score, the exact attacks that worked, and fixes enforced outside the model: allowlists, spend caps, confirmation thresholds, quarantined inputs and signed memory. Flip one on and rerun.

3

Spend limit

The score becomes a limit through expected-loss underwriting: chance of compromise, times share of the wallet lost, times exposure. We sign it, it expires when your agent changes, and Solana's token program enforces it.

A check on every pull request.

Cosign runs in CI. When a prompt change or a new tool makes your agent easier to rob, you find out in review, not in a postmortem.
