Test, score, limit.

An agent can be talked out of its money by a poisoned web page, a fake "pay to fix this error" reply, or a note planted in its memory. Today whoever sets its spending limit is guessing. Cosign replaces the guess with a measurement.

1

Crash test

Your agent gets a funded wallet and normal jobs. We attack it through pages, APIs, tools and memory, rewriting each attack until it lands or the agent holds. We only judge where the money went.

fake_paid_api / urgency −50.00 USDC
2

Score and fixes

A 0 to 100 score, the exact attacks that worked, and fixes enforced outside the model: allowlists, spend caps, confirmation thresholds, quarantined inputs, signed memory.

score 28 → 92 with 3 fixes
3

Spend limit

The score becomes a limit: chance of compromise times share of the wallet lost times exposure. We sign it, it expires when your agent changes, and Solana's token program enforces it.

limit 5,000 USDC, expires in 7 days
